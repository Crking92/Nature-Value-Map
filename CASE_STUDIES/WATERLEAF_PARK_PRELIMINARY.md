### What we can compute without claiming ecosystem benefits were realized

Using only the poster's **reported $700/ac/year reduced mowing expense** and **reported $894/ac seed/herbicide expense**:

- 1-acre *modeled* first-year mowing-only difference after seed/herbicide: **$700 − $894 = −$194**.
- 1-acre *modeled* three-year mowing-only difference after seed/herbicide: **3 × $700 − $894 = $1,206**.
- If **3.6 acres** were actually converted, corresponding modeled totals are **−$698.40** in year 1 and **$4,341.60** accumulated after 3 years, **before omitted labor, fuel, machine, follow-up invasive control, failures/reseeding, discounting or other restoration costs**.
- Poster value of **$28,587.60** for 3.6 acres is **only the arithmetic projection using its unverified full ecosystem-service amount**, not a defensible three-year financial return or verified project benefit.

**Important:** Do not claim that $4,341.60 was booked in the City budget. These are calculations from unverified poster inputs, not an audited cash ledger. Nor can the full prairie total be treated as *incremental* versus the prior grass cover, which also provided some ecological functions. A newly seeded meadow may take multiple growing seasons before reaching the reference condition.

## Other local cost evidence, not attributable to this project

- [City Council, 2025-02-18](https://kyletx.new.swagit.com/videos/335169): approximately **$40–$70/acre/mowing cycle**, city-stated.
- [Hays Free Press, Sept. 2026](https://www.haysfreepress.com/article/26795%2Ckyle-conservation-division-making-headway-in-environmental-change): city division reports about **$800/acre/year** for regular mowing, and broader division totals for two years (about **$74,000** in cost savings, **$231,000** in ecosystem-service value). These broader estimates **are not Waterleaf-specific audited expenditures or measured biophysical outcomes**.

The city also issued a [Parks maintenance RFP](https://www.cityofkyle.com/media/1651) describing different meadow maintenance intensities. The RFP is an operations specification, not a paid invoice.

## Records required to change the evidence level

1. GIS/drill log with actual planted polygon and acreage (**5 assessed vs. 3.6 converted vs. $23,823/7,941 = 3 acres implied**).
2. Herbicide and seed purchase records: product, quantity, unit cost, date, and whether labor, equipment, tractor, drill or fuel costs were included in the **$894/ac**.
3. Year-by-year mowing frequency, labor hours, fully loaded cost and post-conversion costs; explanation of **$720 vs $700**.
4. Exact derivation of **$2,245/ac/year** with source-page/table, dollar year, inflation conversion if any, baseline ecosystem-service value and expected establishment lag.
5. Follow-up vegetation surveys and seed mix with local-native provenance; any updated site condition and supplemental seeding or invasive-control costs.

**Decision for Nature Value Map:** Retain Waterleaf as a **transparent, preliminary local case study**. Do **not** change the site's ecosystem-service coefficients or install $894/ac as a restoration default until records validate scope and completeness.
