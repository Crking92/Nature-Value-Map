Where the number comes from

area × yearly value per acre × health score − optional costs

Example: if the area is 1 acre, the selected value is $1,941 per acre per year, and the health score is 85%, the gross estimate is $1,650 per year before optional costs.

If optional mowing is turned on at $50 per acre per mow and 20 mows per year, the tool subtracts $1,000 per acre per year.
